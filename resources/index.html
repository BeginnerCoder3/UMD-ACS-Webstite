Contribute

Submit a Resource / Study Material

If you built a reaction map, a formula sheet, or a set of practice problems that got you through a course, it will get someone else through it too. Submitted material that we publish here counts toward your chapter service hours.

Send a PDF or a Google Drive link, tell us the course, and we will format it and credit you in the database.

Submit a Resource

What makes a good submission

  • Your own work, or clearly credited if adapted from elsewhere.
  • Tied to a specific UMD course number so we can file it correctly.
  • No exam questions, graded assignments, or anything under a course integrity policy.
  • A PDF, Google Doc, or Google Form link that anyone can open.

These are student-made study aids shared by chapter members. They are not official course material, are not endorsed by any instructor or department, and may not reflect your section's current syllabus. Always check against what your professor assigns.
